# Textual-integrity audit

**Audit date:** 2026-10-06  
**Scope:** `paper/main.tex`, its bibliography, and release-facing research prose.  
**Method:** strongest available local and public-web-assisted review. This is not a Turnitin, iThenticate, Crossref Similarity Check, or private-corpus report, and it cannot support a plagiarism percentage.

## Checks performed

1. Distinctive manuscript phrases were searched verbatim on the public web, including the benchmark name, metric names, “evidence-dominant plan,” the bounded novelty phrase, and the sentence defining the primary pre-execution choice.
2. The abstract and related-work descriptions were compared manually with the primary arXiv, ACL Anthology, ICLR/OpenReview, and proceedings records in `citation_audit.md`.
3. The manuscript was searched for uncited paper titles, copied-looking multi-sentence passages, abrupt voice changes, placeholder language, and claims that reproduce source-specific results without attribution.
4. Bibliographic metadata and named method terminology were separated from original prose because titles, author names, identifiers, and conventional technical phrases are expected to match their sources.
5. The repository's earlier normalized sentence and n-gram audit in `similarity_audit.md` was reviewed, but its old percentages are not treated as a universal similarity score.

## Citation-required boundaries

- Claims about another work's task count, method, results, venue, or scope require a citation and must remain faithful to the primary paper.
- General statements about tool failures may stand without a citation only when presented as motivating possibilities implemented by this benchmark, not as prevalence claims.
- The deterministic counts (96 scenarios, 384 units, 768 no-fault executions, and nine failure types) are generated by this repository and do not require external attribution.
- Prospective power values are simulation outputs from `research/power_analysis.json`; they must never be described as observed model results.

## Conclusion

The public-source and local evidence shows no serious textual-integrity concern in the current manuscript. Expected overlap is limited to bibliography, titles, standard terminology, and attributed method names. No honest universal plagiarism percentage can be supplied because private and subscription corpora were not searched and similarity is not the same as plagiarism.

Before submission, the named author should read and approve every sentence, confirm the AI-assistance disclosure, and—if institutional access exists—run the exact final PDF through an approved similarity service and inspect each match in context. A numeric service score must be reported with the service name, corpus, settings, date, and manual disposition; it must not be invented or inferred from this audit.
